## What you’ll have at the end

<div class="what-you-build">
  <SystemMap variant="full" />
  <SystemMap variant="mini" />
</div>

Your own enterprise at `SUBDOMAIN.ghe.com`, hosted in the region you pick. People sign in with their Microsoft Entra account, Entra keeps users and groups in sync, Azure pays the bill, and developers use Copilot from their IDE or terminal.

## Two paths, same 11 steps

Both paths share every step. Where they differ, the step tells you.

## Who you’ll need

Most steps take one person. A few need a second one for a moment, so book them early. The [journey map](/ghe-launchpad/start/journey-map/) shows exactly when.

## Three things to know before you start

<div class="sticky-row">
  <Sticky color="yellow" title="Your subdomain is forever">
    It becomes `SUBDOMAIN.ghe.com` for everyone and can’t be changed later. Agree on it in [step 0](/ghe-launchpad/steps/prepare/).
  </Sticky>
  <Sticky color="pink" title="No Copilot in the trial">
    Trials don’t include Copilot. You activate the enterprise in [step 4](/ghe-launchpad/steps/activate-enterprise/), then your contact enables Copilot.
  </Sticky>
  <Sticky color="teal" title="Tell your network team early">
    Proxies and firewalls must let `*.SUBDOMAIN.ghe.com` through. The list is in [step 0](/ghe-launchpad/steps/prepare/#6-brief-your-network-team).
  </Sticky>
</div>
