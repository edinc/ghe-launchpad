Copilot isn’t available in a trial. Activating turns your trial into a paid enterprise, so your contact can enable Copilot in step 5. You can do this at any time during the trial.

## Activate

<Steps>

1. Sign in to `https://SUBDOMAIN.ghe.com` as an enterprise owner and open your enterprise account.

2. Click **Activate enterprise**.

3. Choose how to pay: your connected Azure subscription, a credit card or PayPal.

4. Follow the prompts to confirm.

</Steps>

## What you pay for

- **GitHub Enterprise licenses are usage-based.** Each month you pay for the licenses that were actually used, not for a fixed number of seats. A license counts once per person who used it during the month.
- **Who doesn’t use a license:** people in the enterprise who aren’t members of any organization, enterprise owners who aren’t in an organization, and the setup user.
- **Copilot** is billed separately for each person with a license, from step 9 on.

<Callout type="tip" title="Path B: no GitHub Enterprise licenses">
Without organizations, your people stay so-called unaffiliated users. You pay for Copilot Business licenses, not for GitHub Enterprise licenses.
</Callout>
