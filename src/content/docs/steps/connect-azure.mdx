<StepIntro step="connect-azure">
- An enterprise owner signed in to `SUBDOMAIN.ghe.com`; right now that’s the setup user
- The Azure subscription ID ([how to find it](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/azure/azure-portal/get-subscription-tenant-id))
- A Microsoft account with the **Owner** role on that subscription
- An Entra Global Administrator on standby, unless that account may grant tenant-wide consent itself
</StepIntro>

GitHub bills your enterprise’s usage through Azure: licenses, Copilot, Actions minutes and storage. You connect once; after that, it runs on its own.

<BillingFlow />

## Connect the subscription

<ClickPath path="Your enterprise > Billing & Licensing > Payment information" />

<Steps>

1. Scroll to the bottom of **Payment information**. Next to **Metered billing via Azure**, click **Add Azure Subscription**.

2. Sign in with the Microsoft account that has the Owner role on the subscription.

3. Review **Permissions requested** and click **Accept**. This is the admin consent for GitHub’s app in your Entra tenant.

4. Under **Select a subscription**, pick your subscription ID.

5. Tick the box confirming that you want to be billed for metered services through this subscription.

6. Click **Connect**.

</Steps>

<Callout type="note" title="This doesn’t end your trial">
In a trial with managed users, connecting Azure only lets you go beyond the 3,000 included Actions minutes. The trial ends when you activate the enterprise in step 4.
</Callout>

<Callout type="caution" title="Seeing “Need admin approval”?">
The message reads: *GitHub Inc needs permission to access resources in your organization that only an admin can grant.* The account you signed in with can’t consent for your whole Entra tenant. Ask an Entra Global Administrator to sign in during this step, or to [grant tenant-wide admin consent](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/entra/identity/enterprise-apps/grant-admin-consent) for the app or set up an [admin consent workflow](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/entra/identity/enterprise-apps/user-admin-consent-overview#admin-consent-workflow). Then start again from **Add Azure Subscription**.
</Callout>

## Good to know about the bill

<div class="sticky-row">
  <Sticky color="yellow" title="Billed on the 1st">
    Usage from the day you connect shows up on your Azure invoice on the 1st of each month.
  </Sticky>
  <Sticky color="pink" title="No prepaid usage">
    Prepaid usage isn’t available when you pay through Azure.
  </Sticky>
  <Sticky color="teal" title="Several subscriptions?">
    Cost centers let you bill each department, for example, to its own subscription.
  </Sticky>
</div>

## Troubleshooting

<details>
<summary>Your subscription isn’t in the list</summary>

Your account doesn’t have the Owner role on it, or the subscription lives in another tenant. Switch to the right tenant during the Microsoft sign-in, or ask for the Owner role.

</details>

<details>
<summary>The Microsoft sign-in window fails behind your proxy</summary>

Allow `https://github.com/enterprises/oauth_callback`, including all query parameters, while you connect. You can remove it afterwards, but you need it again to change the subscription later.

</details>
