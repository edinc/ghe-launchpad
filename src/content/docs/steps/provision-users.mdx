<StepIntro step="provision-users">
- Single sign-on from step 6
- The personal access token from step 2
- An Entra account with **Application Administrator** or **Cloud Application Administrator**, or an owner of the app
- One test user, and the Entra groups you want in GitHub
</StepIntro>

SCIM creates an account on `SUBDOMAIN.ghe.com` for everyone you assign in Entra ID, keeps names and groups in sync, and removes access when someone leaves. Their usernames end with your shortcode.

## Connect Entra to your enterprise

<ClickPath app="entra" path="Entra ID > Enterprise apps > GitHub Enterprise Managed User (OIDC) > Provisioning" />

<Steps>

1. In the [Microsoft Entra admin center](https://entra.microsoft.com), open **Entra ID** › **Enterprise apps** and select **GitHub Enterprise Managed User (OIDC)**, the app from step 6.

2. Open **Provisioning** and click **+ New configuration**.

3. Under **Admin Credentials**, enter the **Tenant URL** and paste the token from step 2 as **Secret token**:

   ```text "SUBDOMAIN"
   https://api.SUBDOMAIN.ghe.com/scim/v2/enterprises/SUBDOMAIN
   ```

4. Click **Test Connection**, then **Create**.

5. Under **Properties**, add a notification email and turn on accidental deletion prevention, then click **Apply**.

6. Keep the default **Attribute Mapping** unless you have a reason to change it.

</Steps>

## Assign users and groups

<ClickPath app="entra" path="Enterprise apps > GitHub Enterprise Managed User (OIDC) > Users and groups" />

<Steps>

1. Click **Add user/group** and add your test user first.

2. Pick a role for each assignment, for example **Enterprise Owner** for at least one admin and **User** for everyone else. The role becomes the person’s [role in your enterprise](https://docs.github.com/en/enterprise-cloud@latest/admin/managing-accounts-and-repositories/managing-roles-in-your-enterprise/abilities-of-roles).

3. Add the groups whose members should get access.

4. Back in **Provisioning**, click **Start provisioning**.

</Steps>

<Callout type="caution" title="Groups inside groups don’t count">
Entra ID doesn’t provision members of nested groups. Assign the groups that contain your users directly.
</Callout>

## Test with one person

Use [Provision on demand](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/entra/identity/app-provisioning/provision-on-demand) for your test user, so you don’t have to wait for the next sync. Then ask them to sign in at `https://SUBDOMAIN.ghe.com` with their Entra account.

<Callout type="wait" title="Provisioning runs on a schedule">
After the first run, Entra ID syncs changes in regular cycles, not instantly. Follow progress in the provisioning logs.
</Callout>

## Troubleshooting

<details>
<summary>Test Connection fails</summary>

Check the tenant URL: your subdomain appears twice and there’s no trailing text. Make sure the token was copied completely, belongs to the setup user, has the `scim:enterprise` scope and hasn’t been revoked.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Users don’t show up in GitHub</summary>

Check that they’re assigned to the app directly or through a non-nested group, that provisioning is started, and what the provisioning logs say.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Should you turn on “Open SCIM configuration” in GitHub?</summary>

No. That setting is for provisioning through the REST API without a partner identity provider. With Entra ID and OIDC, leave it off.

</details>
