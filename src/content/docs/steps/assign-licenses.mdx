## Assign Copilot Business to your teams

<ClickPath path="Your enterprise > Billing and licensing > Licensing" />

<Steps>

1. Next to **Copilot**, click **Manage**.

2. Open the **Enterprise Teams** tab. To license single people instead, use **All members**.

3. Click **Assign licenses**, search for the team and click **Add licenses**.

</Steps>

People get access to Copilot immediately. Each license is billed per person; licenses added during the month are prorated.

<Callout type="tip" title="Start with a pilot team">
License one small group first, let them try step 10, then add the remaining teams. A removed license is still billed until the end of the billing cycle, with no refund. If the seat is unassigned, Copilot keeps working until then; if it’s revoked, access ends immediately. [How license changes work](https://docs.github.com/en/enterprise-cloud@latest/copilot/reference/copilot-billing/license-changes)
</Callout>

## Path A: Copilot Enterprise

Assigning licenses directly to teams and people gives them **Copilot Business**. For **Copilot Enterprise**, enable Copilot for specific organizations instead: on the same Copilot page, under organization access, allow specific organizations and choose the plan for each.
