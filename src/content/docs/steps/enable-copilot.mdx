For enterprises on GHE.com, Copilot is switched on from GitHub’s side. You ask for it, then set your rules while you wait.

## 1. Ask your contact

Send your Microsoft or GitHub contact a short note with your company name, your `SUBDOMAIN.ghe.com` address, the fact that the enterprise is activated, and roughly how many people will use Copilot. Your contact enables Copilot for your enterprise and tells you when it’s done.

<Callout type="wait" title="Don’t wait idle">
Continue with single sign-on (step 6), provisioning (step 7) and teams (step 8) in the meantime.
</Callout>

## 2. Check that Copilot arrived

<ClickPath path="Your enterprise > Billing and licensing > Licensing" />

You should see **Copilot** with a **Manage** button. If the page says **Contact support to enable**, or the Copilot card or **Manage** button is missing, Copilot isn’t enabled yet; let your contact know.

## 3. Set the Copilot policies

Decide what Copilot may do before anyone gets a license.

<ClickPath path="Your enterprise > AI controls > Copilot" />

<Steps>

1. Set **Policies for enterprise-assigned users**. They apply to everyone who gets Copilot directly from the enterprise, which is how you’ll assign licenses in step 9.

2. In the **Privacy** section, decide whether to allow **Suggestions matching public code**. It’s allowed by default.

3. Open **Configure features & clients** and choose the features, IDEs and models you want to offer.

4. Check **Agents** and **MCP** in the sidebar for the policies on Copilot agents and MCP servers.

</Steps>

<Callout type="note" title="Path A: organizations can have their own rules">
For policies you set to let organizations decide, each organization chooses its own setting. If someone belongs to several organizations, the least restrictive setting wins.
</Callout>

## 4. Let Copilot through the network

On GHE.com, Copilot only needs your own addresses. Make sure your proxy or firewall allows them:

```text "SUBDOMAIN"
https://SUBDOMAIN.ghe.com
https://*.SUBDOMAIN.ghe.com
```

Allow `https://origin-tracker.githubusercontent.com` too if you want code referencing for suggestions that match public code.
