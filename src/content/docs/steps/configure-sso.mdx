With OIDC, people sign in to `SUBDOMAIN.ghe.com` with their Entra account, and your Conditional Access policies apply automatically. This step turns sign-in on; step 7 brings the people.

<IdentityFlow />

## Turn on OIDC

<ClickPath path="Your enterprise > Identity provider > Single sign-on configuration" />

<Steps>

1. Sign in to `https://SUBDOMAIN.ghe.com` as the setup user `SHORTCODE_admin` and open your enterprise.

2. Click **Identity provider**, then **Single sign-on configuration**.

3. Under **OIDC single sign-on**, select **Enable OIDC configuration** and click **Save**.

4. GitHub sends you to Microsoft. Sign in with the Global Administrator account.

5. Tick **Consent on behalf of your organization** and click **Accept**. This adds the **GitHub Enterprise Managed User (OIDC)** app to your Entra tenant.

6. Back in GitHub, save the SSO recovery codes with **Download**, **Print** or **Copy**, and store them in the password manager.

7. Click **Enable OIDC Authentication**.

</Steps>

<Callout type="note" title="Nobody can sign in yet">
Single sign-on is on, but your people don’t have accounts yet. They arrive with provisioning in step 7. The setup user keeps signing in with its password.
</Callout>

## Good to know

<div class="sticky-row">
  <Sticky color="teal" title="Conditional Access is automatic">
    With OIDC, your Entra Conditional Access policies apply without an extra switch. Optional: extended protection for web sessions (in preview) under **Authentication security**.
  </Sticky>
  <Sticky color="yellow" title="Sign-in starts at GitHub">
    OIDC doesn’t support signing in from the Entra side, so people open `https://SUBDOMAIN.ghe.com` instead of clicking an app tile.
  </Sticky>
</div>

## Troubleshooting

<details>
<summary>Microsoft says you need admin approval</summary>

The account you signed in with isn’t a Global Administrator. Repeat the step with one, or have your Entra admin sign in for you.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Your tenant already uses OIDC for another GitHub enterprise</summary>

Each Entra tenant supports one OIDC connection. Use [SAML](https://docs.github.com/en/enterprise-cloud@latest/admin/managing-iam/configuring-authentication-for-enterprise-managed-users/configuring-saml-single-sign-on-for-enterprise-managed-users) for this enterprise instead.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Nobody can sign in after a change to single sign-on</summary>

Sign in as the setup user with password and second factor and fix the configuration. If that’s not possible, use one of the [SSO recovery codes](https://docs.github.com/en/enterprise-cloud@latest/admin/managing-iam/managing-recovery-codes-for-your-enterprise/downloading-your-enterprise-accounts-single-sign-on-recovery-codes).

</details>
